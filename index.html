0.16 hRound 1 — Fused RoPE and the unpadded fast path
# MiniCPM sparse prefill optimization

The initial selection is a copy of the supplied stock tree. The quick reference times 16,384 tokens at about 2.1 seconds. The baseline profiler runs on all eight A100s at the prescribed batch size of one. The frozen judge requires exact equality for its hidden-position outputs and forced response probabilities.

The upstream rotary embedding promotes the full projections to FP32 and materializes both halves, products and sums. A fused implementation can read each pair once, use separate FP32 products and additions, then write the original BF16 result. The sparse path also gathers Q/K/V and scatters the result for all-one masks. Bypassing that packing when the mask is verified all ones preserves the attention inputs and indices.

The 16K stock profile took 2.112 s median. GEMMs accounted for 896 ms of GPU time and sparse FlashAttention for 631 ms. Relevant eager operators included 115 ms of multiplication, 109 ms of copies, 25 ms sorting indices, and 20 ms squaring. The operator table does not expose launch grids or hardware counters.

First candidate keeps the existing FP32 RMS mean and reciprocal square root, fuses only elementwise operations around them, fuses rotary products with FMA contraction disabled, and sorts the original top-k selection in a Triton kernel that also applies the causal mask and casts to int32. A guarded single-request unpadded path sends sequence-major query, key and value tensors directly to the stock sparse attention. Padded requests use the original packing code.

The first candidate's submitted quick arm took 1.894 s median. Its fingerprints and every response array matched the supplied quick reference on all eight ranks. Promoted minicpm in FINAL.json on this evidence while the independent stock replay runs.

Quick frozen check passed at 1.11399× (reference 2.10974 s). Across 64 forced tokens, the maximum log-probability difference was zero and the top-128 check was exact. Reference drift was 0.0077%, and the judge reported no competing GPU process. The full before/submitted/replay run is in runs/full-first. The candidate source hashes are 3195cc45aeb3e4c2e274369a5eb621a3b8e7bcbf3f0ef0775b6dea4bf56a4b49 (modeling_minicpm.py) and aa16da15ea464f40b9bd610e9e18255cd53461e81ded84d873845d3cb2bcf079 (prefill_ops.py).

## Sparse score producer and mask

The first B variant emitted 16 pooled BF16 values and one halo per 64 fine keys into global memory. At 65k, score storage dropped from 1,073,725,440 to 285,208,320 bytes, but every rank and length slowed end to end. The fused variant keeps the tile values in CTA shared memory, uses the original MMA and head-sum order, and applies the exact BF16 threshold, ascending-index tie rule and causal mask before emitting packed words. At 65k the four-warp CTA uses 8,704 bytes for its pooled rows. [Compact probe](runs/b-mask/output) · [Register and grouping sweep](runs/tune-pruned/output)

The final kernel specializes the unpadded traversal, tracks written tiles, omits fine tiles that can feed only forced or future blocks, and pipelines two K tiles when the four-CTA shared-memory budget permits. A build that allowed probability multiplication to contract across a head addition changed one synthetic mask row and two held-out model rows. It was excluded. Explicit FP32 rounding at that addition fixed all three. In the buffered kernel for 512 blocks, the correction replaced 20 FFMA instructions with 20 FMUL and 20 FADD instructions. ## Experiments

- `runs/gemm-study` screens cuBLASLt heuristics, alternate operand orientations, and pretransposed weights for four projection shapes. Each accepted output was compared with torch.mm. On the large gate, up, and down GEMMs the best verified algorithms had times close to the default. The narrow 256-channel projection had an isolated gain (0.168 versus 0.210 ms). The largest GEMMs did not justify replacing ATen.
- `runs/warp-study`, `runs/warp-tune`, and `runs/hybrid` implement independent descending block traversal by warp. K and V are repacked into contiguous Ampere MMA register fragments. Experiments varied CTA size, read-only caching, multiplication order of independent output tiles, and pipelined shared memory for blocks selected by all queries. An early branch skipping both denominator and output scaling changed 160 BF16 results on a 16K operator case. Keeping denominator scaling in the original expression preserved rounding. The eight-warp variant gave 7.43 ms versus 7.52 ms on a synthetic mask. Two wide shared-memory variants exceeded launch resources and were discarded.
- The first complete model candidate combines that warp implementation with grouped-layout RoPE and selected final attention output, retaining all keys, values and compressed cache state. `candidates/packed` contains the actual binary. Frozen quick check passed exact logit fingerprints and 64 forced token steps, but 1.31571 seconds at 16K was slower than the starting tree. See `runs/packed-quick/check.json`.
- `runs/cutlass-study` interleaves gate and up projection rows, rounds both register accumulators to BF16, and computes SiLU and multiplication in the GEMM epilogue. All eight variants were exact on a 16K random projection. Best 17.94 ms, separate ATen GEMMs plus incumbent SiLU 18.49 ms in this isolated run.
- `runs/rms-study` tests vectorized BF16 pairs in residual RMSNorm while preserving all 128 original FP32 accumulation subsequences. Variant 1 matched both residual and normalized tensors on random data, reducing the residual operator from 0.600 to 0.365 ms at 16K and 2.314 to 1.410 ms at 65K. Variant 4 exceeded available registers per CTA. Its apparent timing is invalid.

The layout candidate emits grouped Q directly from RoPE, uses the incumbent full attention kernel, and uses packed sparse attention and a full-K ordered O projection for up to 128 requested final positions. Full-length masks and the complete KV cache are still built. The frozen complete model time arm measured 1.28196, 2.68771 and 5.65352 seconds. All eight ranks' hidden-position fingerprints, first tokens, response IDs, response log probabilities and top-128 arrays were compared directly with the incumbent using `python runs/compare.py runs/incumbent-before/probe runs/layout-full-time/probe`. See `runs/layout-full-time/incumbent-comparison.json`. The candidate with its binary is frozen in `candidates/layout`.

### Fusing residual projections

The O projection reads the grouped attention output in logical contraction order. Its epilogue rounds to BF16, scales, rounds again, and adds the residual. The down projection uses the same epilogue. The full frozen stock check on `candidates/outputdown` passed at **1.681924×** original, with medians 1.266668, 2.666837 and 5.597480 seconds. Reference drift was 0.427%. All 1,536 sampled response log probabilities were identical and all top-128 results matched. The direct comparison to the fresh starting tree was **1.025877×**. Evidence is in `runs/outputdown-stock/check.json` and `runs/outputdown-stock/incumbent-comparison.json`.

Additional experiments used the same random full-model input and separate kernel fixtures. Packing the QKV weights with torch.cat did not improve full-model time. Computing just the requested final query tiles preserved both random-position fingerprints and full KV state but produced 1.275 and 5.547 seconds on the 16K and 65K random local screen. Merging K and V recovered most of that cost. FlashAttention tile variants, independent per-warp traversal, two to four cooperating warps per query, and preswizzled shared-memory pipelines were explored on saved model activations and masks from layers 9 and 23. Most lost to the grouped native path. See `runs/flash-variants`, `runs/team-attn`, `runs/tiled-attn` and `runs/mma-pipeline`. A hand-written packed dual gate/up GEMM was exact but slower. The larger CUTLASS gate/up tile sweep and 118 cuBLASLt algorithms failed to establish a robust gain on the large MLP projections. A fused second projection and SiLU product measured 9.59 versus 9.58 ms at 16K and 38.12 versus 38.60 ms at 65K. Transposed MLP intermediates also gave mixed results. The sources and timing logs are in `runs/packed-gemm`, `runs/cutlass-tune`, `runs/lt-exhaustive`, `runs/upgate` and `runs/mlp-layout`.

### Joint QKV and gated MLP kernels

The QKV projection uses a shared CUTLASS MMA main loop for Q, K and V weight tiles, then rotates the rounded Q and K fragments and writes their native attention layouts. The full time arm in `runs/rotproj-time` matched the incumbent's fingerprints and response arrays. Its three medians were 1.26245, 2.65751 and 5.59957 seconds, or 1.028089× the fresh incumbent.

The paired MLP reads the original gate and up weights in alternating eight-column groups inside one GEMM. Both accumulators round to BF16 before SiLU and multiplication. The output feeds the fused residual down projection. It avoids packing the weights and writing either full-size intermediate. Native tests for the 256×128 tile were bitwise equal at 16K and 65K, with 17.78 and 71.64 ms against 18.05–18.42 and 72.59–72.87 ms for separate projections. The full `candidates/paired` arm measured 1.26019, 2.65599 and 5.54762 seconds, 1.032103× the incumbent. All eight ranks matched and were uncontended. Source and operator tests are in `runs/gate-pairs`.

The full frozen stock verdict for `candidates/paired` was **PASS, 1.692399×**, with 0.104% reference drift, zero response log-probability difference on 1,536 tokens and identical top-128 results. See `runs/paired-stock/check.json`.

### Final configuration sweep

The next gate kernel preselects the original gate and up weight pointers for each lane before the MMA main loop. Its output epilogue uses the BF16 product and a correctly rounded division for very large SiLU denominators. The SiLU arithmetic was screened over all 65,536 BF16 inputs in `runs/silu-exact`. A two-stage shared-memory sparse-attention pipeline was tested on grouped Q and grouped output. On a layer-23 fixture the best variant was bitwise exact and took 8.316 versus 8.461 ms at 16K and 42.787 versus 43.454 ms at 65K. Other pipeline depths, warp counts and traversal orders were slower. More specialized load iterators and CTA tile orders for O, down and QKV had mixed or negative results, so those replacements were omitted. See `runs/attn-tune`, `runs/gate-iterator`, `runs/raster-gemm` and `runs/qkv-iterator`.

`candidates/tuned` incorporates the improved gate iterator, the grouped sparse pipeline, vector RMSNorm, and compact last-layer query tiles. Every requested tile includes its four original query rows to preserve block scoring. The model constructs and retains the complete keys, values and compressed caches at every layer. Its full timing arm in `runs/tuned-time` verified all eight ranks against the incumbent at 1.260798, 2.654509 and 5.536008 seconds (**1.032849×** the incumbent). In an additional 32K random full-model screen, all seven configurations returned the same nonmonotone-position fingerprint. `candidates/native` measured 2.61528 seconds, with the native grouped full-sequence attention and per-request sharing of compression counts. It directly views the RoPE tables when the model generated positions from zero. Explicit positions still gather from the tables. Its complete stock check is in `runs/native-stock`.

For an additional stock comparison on `candidates/tuned`, the final stock replay from the paired check and the initial stock arm from the native check enclose its frozen timing arm. `python runs/bracket_timing.py runs/paired-stock/replay runs/tuned-time/probe runs/native-stock/before` gives **1.694419×** original, with 0.423% maximum stock drift. All three arms have identical first tokens, hidden-position hashes and response arrays; every recorded sample had one GPU process. This is an auxiliary comparison of existing arms. The standalone full-check verdicts are recorded separately.

The complete native candidate timing arm matched all eight incumbent ranks, with medians 1.256259, 2.654102 and 5.541788 seconds. Its directly measured geometric mean was **1.033785× the fresh starting tree**, ahead of the pipelined candidate. FINAL.json was updated to `candidates/native` on that evidence.
